01Work

2026 · Legal tech · Local LLM

NyayaSathi

A study assistant for Indian law students that runs on a laptop and cites its sources.

Law students using ChatGPT get confident answers with made-up case citations. NyayaSathi takes plain words like “cheque bounce” or “land acquisition” and returns the actual statute sections and the leading Supreme Court judgments, read from Indian Kanoon.

The design rule: the model never decides what the law is. It only turns your words into search keywords and summarises text it has been given. Section lookup, the old-to-new law mapping and citation checks are plain code, so they can't hallucinate.

  • 3,300+sections parsed from 10 major Acts
  • IPC 420 → BNS 318(4)old-to-new criminal law mapping, down to the sub-section
  • 4 verdictsa citation is verified, wrong for that case, belongs to another case, or not found

Python · FastAPI · Ollama (Qwen3 8B) · Indian Kanoon · vanilla JS

Computer vision · Safety

Helmet Monitoring & Face Recognition

Spots helmet violations on a construction site and works out who they belong to, in real time.

YOLOv8 finds people and helmets. A bounding-box check decides whether the helmet is actually on the head or just nearby, and InsightFace matches the face against known workers using normalised embeddings and cosine similarity. Frames are streamed and processed asynchronously over WebSockets, so the dashboard stays live.

  • 96.1%mAP@0.5 for helmet detection
  • 69.2%mAP@0.5:0.95
  • 1 papera survey of the approaches, published in IJARSCT

YOLOv8 · InsightFace · OpenCV · FastAPI · WebSockets
